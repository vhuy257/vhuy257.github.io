## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Huy Nguyễn for a frontend engineering role. Their job is to assess craft, relevant experience, and whether to advance toward an interview or outreach.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that proves React/Next.js frontend craft through live demos and showcase website-template / landing-page work. Success means a hiring evaluator can quickly verify skills via real demos, scan employers and projects, and start contact.

## Positioning

Evidence-led portfolio: live project demos and template landing pages do the proving, not claims alone. Neighboring “generic frontend portfolio” sites cannot truthfully substitute those demos and product surfaces.

## Operating Context

Single-page home flow (about → experience → projects → contact) plus collections browsing by category. Visitors open live demos, review work history, and reach out via the contact form or listed channels. Deployed as a public site at https://vhuy257.github.io.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Profile introduction, skills, work experience, project collections, and contact form.
- Project categories in use: web templates and CMS dashboards (technical-tips category exists in data but is hidden from filters/previews).
- Contact via Formspree-backed form and email / LinkedIn / GitHub links.
- Stack is the existing Next.js App Router + React codebase; not greenfield.
- Undecided: none recorded beyond normal content maintenance.

1. Prove craft with live demos and real template work, not empty claims.
2. Keep hiring evaluators’ scan path short: who you are, where you worked, what you shipped, how to reach you.
